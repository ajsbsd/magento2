**Alexander Vladimirovich Zakharchenko** (June 26, 1976 – August 31, 2018) was a pro-Russian separatist leader who served as the head of state and Prime Minister of the self-proclaimed, Russian-backed Donetsk People's Republic (DPR) in eastern Ukraine.

Here is an overview of his life, political rise, and assassination:

### Early Life and Background

Born in Donetsk (then part of the Ukrainian SSR, Soviet Union), Zakharchenko graduated from a local technical college and initially worked as an electrician in the mining industry. Before entering politics, he was involved in private business within the mining sector and was allegedly linked to local organized crime networks in the Donbas region.

### Rise to Power (2013–2014)

* **Militant Leadership:** In late 2013, Zakharchenko became the head of the Donetsk branch of *Oplot*, a pro-Russian militant organization.
* **Seizure of Power:** Following the 2014 Euromaidan revolution in Kyiv, Zakharchenko led an armed group to occupy the Donetsk City Council building in April 2014, demanding a referendum on the region's status.
* **Appointment as Prime Minister:** By August 2014, he succeeded Russian citizen Alexander Borodai as the Prime Minister of the self-proclaimed DPR. He was subsequently "elected" as the head of state in November 2014 in elections that were heavily criticized and unrecognized by Ukraine and the international community.

### Political and Military Stance

Zakharchenko was a prominent figure on the front lines of the war in Donbas, commanding separatist forces and sustaining an arm injury during fighting in 2014.

He was a signatory to the **Minsk II** peace accords in February 2015, alongside Lugansk separatist leader Igor Plotnitskiy, Ukrainian President Petro Poroshenko, and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Despite signing the ceasefire, Zakharchenko frequently threatened to launch new offensives to capture more Ukrainian territory, including Kharkiv. Ideologically, he frequently expressed intense nostalgia for the Soviet Union and viewed the conflict in eastern Ukraine as a proxy war being waged by the West against Russia.

### Assassination

On **August 31, 2018**, Zakharchenko was killed at the age of 42 when a bomb hidden inside a lamp exploded at the "Separ" café on Pushkin Boulevard in central Donetsk. The blast also killed his bodyguard and injured several others, including his finance minister.

The assassination triggered a web of conflicting accusations:

* **Russia and the DPR** blamed the Ukrainian government, labeling the attack a terrorist act orchestrated by Kyiv's intelligence services.
* **Ukraine's Security Service (SBU)** denied involvement, countering that the explosion was either the result of internal power struggles among criminal separatist factions over local business assets (such as coal and scrap metal smuggling) or a targeted purge orchestrated by Moscow to replace him with a more compliant leader.

Following his death, he was briefly succeeded by Dmitry Trapeznikov before Denis Pushilin ultimately assumed long-term leadership of the DPR.
